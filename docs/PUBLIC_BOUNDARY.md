# Public Boundary

Builder Lab is public, but not everything belongs here.

## Public

- Reproducible experiment plans
- Hardware setup notes
- Non-sensitive configs
- Run logs that do not reveal private credentials or partner data
- Media from experiments that are safe to share
- Negative results and setup failures

## Private

- Credentials, tokens, keys, and account details
- Private lab or partner data
- Unreleased paper claims
- Private purchasing or personal information
- Anything that implies clinical, safety-critical, or product deployment

## Rule

When unsure, keep it private until it can be described as a reproducible
experiment artifact rather than a private note.
